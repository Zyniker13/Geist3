# COMPETITORS — Real-Device Test Sheet

Purpose: reproducible on-device checklist for the eight competitor apps named in ROADMAP P0.7. Filled by Mahler or a reviewer on real devices; GLM does not install apps or create accounts. Findings here are observations, not review quotes (RESEARCH.md §6: research hypotheses, not a representative study).

## Instructions

1. One device per pass where possible (an iPhone and an iPad; a Mac or Apple TV where the app supports them). Record device, OS, and app version in each app's header row.
2. Do a cold start (kill the app, then launch) for every timing measurement. Repeat the time-to-first-card measurement three times and record the median.
3. Tap counts: count every tap from cold launch to the first card actually played (not merely selected). Also count taps for undo and for ending a deal and returning to the game list.
4. Accessibility audit: turn on VoiceOver and try one full deal; note what is spoken, whether private cards are spoken on the private device (and whether the app warns about speaker audio), target sizes, contrast, Dynamic Type behavior, and whether a full deal is completable with VoiceOver or keyboard alone.
5. Network behavior: note whether the app works offline, how it finds other players (Wi-Fi discovery, QR, account-based matchmaking), what happens when the connection drops mid-deal, and whether ads appear and how often.
6. Record "n/a" rather than guessing. A blank cell means "not yet tested"; do not leave cells blank after a pass.
7. Date each pass. Re-test after major app updates.
